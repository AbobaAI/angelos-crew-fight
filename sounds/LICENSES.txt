crew-fight sounds — licence record
==================================
All files in this folder (*.wav) were synthesised locally with ffmpeg's aevalsrc / anoisesrc
generators by sounds/gen.sh (sine/noise maths, filters, normalisation). No third-party
samples, recordings or sound packs are used.
